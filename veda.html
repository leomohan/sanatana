🕉️ The Four Vedas

Veda 1

📖 Rigveda ऋग्वेद

The oldest of the four Vedas, a collection of 1,028 hymns (suktas) in 10 mandalas. It praises deities like Agni, Indra and Varuna, and contains the famous Gayatri Mantra and Nasadiya Sukta on creation.

Veda 2

🎵 Samaveda सामवेद

The Veda of melodies and chants. It takes verses from the Rigveda and sets them to musical notation (saman), forming the root of Indian classical music and Vedic ritual singing.

Veda 3

🔥 Yajurveda यजुर्वेद

The Veda of sacrifice (yajna). It contains prose formulas and procedures for performing rituals, guiding the adhvaryu priest. Divided into Shukla (white) and Krishna (black) Yajurveda.

Veda 4

🌿 Atharvaveda अथर्ववेद

The Veda of everyday life — medicine, spells, charms, kingship and cosmology. It deals with healing, protection and the practical concerns of ancient society, later linked to Ayurveda.

✨ Principal Upanishads (Mukhya Upanishads)

Ishavasya ईशावास्य

Shukla Yajurveda

A short text of 18 verses teaching that the Divine pervades all — one should enjoy through renunciation. A bridge between ritual and philosophy.

🧠 Philosophy

Kena केन

Samaveda

Asks “By whom is the mind directed?” — explores Brahman as the unseen seer, the unheard hearer, beyond all description.

🧠 Philosophy

Katha कठ

Krishna Yajurveda

The dialogue of Nachiketa and Yama (death) on the nature of the Self and immortality — famous for the chariot metaphor of body and soul.

🧠 Philosophy

Prashna प्रश्न

Atharvaveda

Six seekers ask sage Pippalada six questions about creation, prana, dream states and the Self — structured as a Q&A.

🧠 Philosophy🌌 Cosmology

Mundaka मुण्डक

Atharvaveda

Distinguishes “lower knowledge” (ritual texts) from “higher knowledge” (knowledge of Brahman). Uses the arrow-and-bow metaphor for concentration.

🧠 Philosophy

Mandukya माण्डूक्य

Atharvaveda

The shortest Upanishad (12 verses) on the sacred syllable Om and the four states of consciousness, including turiya, the fourth.

🧠 Philosophy

Taittiriya तैत्तिरीय

Krishna Yajurveda

Lays out the five sheaths (koshas) of the self and the famous “satyam jnanam anantam brahma” — truth, knowledge, infinity.

🧠 Philosophy

Aitareya ऐतरेय

Rigveda

Explores creation and the entry of the Self into the body, with the celebrated teaching “Consciousness is Brahman” (Prajnanam Brahma).

🧠 Philosophy🌌 Cosmology

Chandogya छान्दोग्य

Samaveda

One of the oldest and largest Upanishads, home of “Tat Tvam Asi” (Thou art That) and the story of Satyakama and the syllable Om.

🧠 Philosophy🙏 Devotion

Brihadaranyaka बृहदारण्यक

Shukla Yajurveda

The largest Upanishad, containing the “neti neti” (not this, not this) method and the great saying “Aham Brahmasmi” (I am Brahman).

🧠 Philosophy🌌 Cosmology

Shvetashvatara श्वेताश्वतर

Krishna Yajurveda

Presents a personal God (Ishvara) and devotion (bhakti) alongside Vedanta philosophy — a synthesis of theism and monism.

🧠 Philosophy🙏 Devotion

📜 The Mahapuranas (a selection of 9 of the 18)

Brahma Purana ब्रह्मपुराण

Describes creation by Brahma, genealogies, the sacred sites of Puri and Konark, and the glory of the Sun god. One of the eighteen Mahapuranas.

🌌 Cosmology🙏 Devotion

Vishnu Purana विष्णुपुराण

Focuses on Vishnu and his avatars, cosmology, the Manvantaras and the genealogy of kings. Famous for the story of Prahlada and Dhruva.

🙏 Devotion🌌 Cosmology

Shiva Purana शिवपुराण

Devoted to Shiva — his forms, the twelve Jyotirlingas, the marriage to Parvati, and the destruction of the demon Tripura.

🙏 Devotion

Bhagavata Purana भागवतपुराण

The most celebrated Purana, narrating Krishna’s life, the Dashavatara, and the child-devotee Prahlada. It is the supreme text of Vaishnava bhakti.

🙏 Devotion🧠 Philosophy

Markandeya Purana मार्कण्डेयपुराण

Contains the Devi Mahatmya (Durga Saptashati), the great hymn to the Goddess, celebrating her victory over Mahishasura.

🙏 Devotion

Matsya Purana मत्स्यपुराण

Narrates the fish avatar (Matsya) of Vishnu who saves the Vedas and Manu from the great deluge, plus temple architecture and king lists.

🌌 Cosmology🙏 Devotion

Agni Purana अग्निपुराण

A vast encyclopedia-like text covering ritual, iconography, medicine, astronomy, grammar and the art of war — a compendium of knowledge.

🔥 Ritual🌌 Cosmology

Linga Purana लिङ्गपुराण

Centers on the Shiva Linga as the cosmic principle and the source of creation, with detailed ritual and the significance of pilgrimage.

🙏 Devotion🔥 Ritual

Skanda Purana स्कन्दपुराण

The largest Purana, dedicated to Skanda (Kartikeya). It extols the sacred geography of India, with long sections on Kashi, Avanti (Ujjain), Prabhasa, Puri and other tirthas.

🙏 Devotion

🔵 Brahmanas & Aranyakas

Aitareya Brahmana ऐतरेय ब्राह्मण

Belongs to the Rigveda. Explains the procedure of Soma sacrifices and the famous legend of Shunahshepa, and the ideal of the king as protector.

🔥 Ritual

Shatapatha Brahmana शतपथ ब्राह्मण

The largest and most important Brahmana (Yajurveda). Details the agnicayana fire altar, creation myths, and contains the earliest Upanishadic thought.

🔥 Ritual🌌 Cosmology

Jaiminiya Brahmana जैमिनीय ब्राह्मण

Belongs to the Samaveda. Deals with Soma rituals, chants and stories of sages, reflecting the musical and sacrificial tradition.

🔥 Ritual

Gopatha Brahmana गोपथ ब्राह्मण

The only surviving Brahmana of the Atharvaveda, describing ritual applications of Atharvan mantras and the duties of the priests.

🔥 Ritual

Aitareya Aranyaka ऐतरेय आरण्यक

A “forest text” of the Rigveda, exploring the mystic meaning of sacrifice and the cosmic person — it leads directly into the Aitareya Upanishad.

🧠 Philosophy🔥 Ritual

Brihadaranyaka बृहदारण्यक

Technically both an Aranyaka and Upanishad. A profound dialogue text on the Self, creation, and the “neti neti” path to Brahman.

🧠 Philosophy🌌 Cosmology

Taittiriya Aranyaka तैत्तिरीय आरण्यक

Part of the Krishna Yajurveda. Contains the famous Narayana Sukta and leads into the Taittiriya Upanishad's teachings on the sheaths.

🧠 Philosophy🔥 Ritual

Jaiminiya Upanishad Brahmana जैमिनीय उपनिषद् ब्राह्मण

Belongs to the Samaveda (Jaiminiya school) and is also called the Talavakara Aranyaka. It explores the esoteric meaning of the Sama chants and meditation (upasana) on their syllables, and contains the Kena Upanishad.

🧠 Philosophy🔥 Ritual

⚔️ The Itihasas (Epics)

🏹 Ramayana रामायण

By Valmiki · 24,000 verses

The journey of Prince Rama of Ayodhya — his exile, the abduction of Sita by Ravana, and the war to rescue her with Hanuman’s help. A foundational text of dharma, loyalty and ideal kingship.

⚔️ Epic🙏 Devotion🧠 Philosophy

🏛️ Mahabharata महाभारत

By Vyasa · ~100,000 verses

One of the longest epics ever composed — the Kurukshetra war between the Pandavas and Kauravas. It contains the Bhagavad Gita and explores duty, choice, fate and the moral complexity of human life.

⚔️ Epic🧠 Philosophy🙏 Devotion

🪷 Bhagavad Gita भगवद्गीता

Part of Mahabharata · 700 verses

Krishna’s dialogue with Arjuna on the battlefield — the essence of Vedanta in 18 chapters. It teaches karma yoga, jnana yoga and bhakti yoga as paths to liberation.

🧠 Philosophy🙏 Devotion

🐄 Harivamsha हरिवंश

Appendix to Mahabharata

An appendix to the Mahabharata that narrates the life of Krishna, his lineage and his childhood in Vrindavan — a bridge between epic and Puranic tradition.

⚔️ Epic🙏 Devotion

🌿 The Upavedas (Applied Knowledge)

🌿 Ayurveda आयुर्वेद

Upaveda of Atharvaveda (per Charaka & Sushruta)

The science of life — a holistic system of medicine covering diagnosis, herbal remedies, diet, daily routine and surgery. Rooted in the balance of three doshas: vata, pitta and kapha.

🌿 Healing

🏹 Dhanurveda धनुर्वेद

Upaveda of Yajurveda

The Veda of archery and warfare — training, ethics of battle, weapons and strategy. Its principles echo throughout the Mahabharata and Ramayana.

⚔️ Epic🔥 Ritual

🎶 Gandharvaveda गान्धर्ववेद

Upaveda of Samaveda

The Veda of music and dance — ragas, performance arts, and the spiritual power of sound. It expands the melodic tradition of the Samaveda into a complete aesthetic science.

🙏 Devotion

⚖️ Arthashastra अर्थशास्त्र

Upaveda of Atharvaveda (per the Charanavyuha)

The science of statecraft, economics and governance. Attributed to Chanakya, it covers diplomacy, taxation, military strategy and the ethics of a ruler — a practical guide to power.
