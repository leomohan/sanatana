Navagraha Temples

Nine temples in the Kaveri delta around Kumbakonam, each the seat of one of the nine Grahas of Jyotisha. Except at Suryanar Kovil, the main deity is Shiva and the Graha has its own shrine within the temple. Built and enlarged under the Cholas, they are visited together as one pilgrimage.

Why nine temples?

A temple legend tells of the sage Kalava, whom the nine Grahas cured of illness. Brahma, angered that the planets had granted a boon on their own, cursed them with leprosy and sent them to Vellerukku Vanam — today's Suryanar Kovil. Shiva lifted the curse and declared that the Grahas would bless devotees from these places. Most pilgrims base themselves in Kumbakonam and cover all nine over one or two days.

1

Surya · Suryanar Kovil சூரியன் · சூரியனார் கோயில்

The Sun

The one Navagraha temple where the planet itself is the presiding deity: Surya stands in the main shrine, with the other eight Grahas in their own shrines around him. Pilgrims usually begin the circuit here. Legend makes this the place, once called Vellerukku Vanam, where the nine Grahas were cursed by Brahma for granting the sage Kalava a boon, and later freed by Shiva.

Chandra · Thingalur சந்திரன் · திங்களூர்

The Moon

A Shiva temple near Thiruvaiyaru whose very name comes from thingal, the Tamil word for the Moon; Chandra is said to have regained his lustre by worshipping here. It is linked with the Nayanmar Appudhi Adigal, whose story Sekkizhar tells in the Periya Puranam, and is a favoured place for a child's first rice feeding (Annaprashanam).

Angaraka · Vaitheeswaran Koil செவ்வாய் · வைத்தீஸ்வரன் கோயில்

Mars

Here Shiva is Vaidyanathar, the divine physician, who is said to have cured Angaraka (Mars) of a skin disease. Devotees come for healing as much as for Mars, and the town is the best-known centre of Nadi astrology, read from palm-leaf manuscripts.

Budha · Thiruvenkadu புதன் · திருவெண்காடு

Mercury

The "white forest" temple of Swetharanyeswarar on the road to Poompuhar, with a separate shrine for Budha, the Graha of speech and intellect. It is also famed for Shiva's fierce Aghora form, and its three sacred tanks are believed to bring relief to those who bathe in all of them.

5

Guru · Alangudi குரு · ஆலங்குடி

Jupiter

Guru is worshipped here in the form of Dakshinamurthy, Shiva as the silent teacher. The village name is read as alam (poison) + kudi (drank), recalling Shiva swallowing the poison from the churning of the ocean — hence Apatsahayesvarar, the one who saves in times of danger. Crowds peak at Guru Peyarchi, Jupiter's transit into a new sign.

Shukra · Kanjanur சுக்கிரன் · கஞ்சனூர்

Venus

Unusually, there is no separate shrine for Shukra: Shiva himself, as Agniswarar, is worshipped as Venus, and Shukra is said to reside within the lingam. Agni is believed to have worshipped Shiva here, giving the deity his name. The temple stands on the northern bank of the Kaveri.

Shani · Thirunallar சனி · திருநள்ளாறு

Saturn

One of the busiest temples of the circuit, in the Karaikal enclave of Puducherry. Tradition holds that King Nala was freed from Shani's long affliction after bathing in the temple tank, the Nala Theertham, which gives the town its name. Sani Peyarchi, Saturn's move into a new sign every two and a half years, draws vast crowds. It is a Paadal Petra Sthalam sung in the Tevaram.

8

Rahu · Thirunageswaram ராகு · திருநாகேஸ்வரம்

North lunar node

Rahu, said to have worshipped Shiva here to be freed from a curse, appears in an unusual auspicious form with a human face and his two consorts, Nagavalli and Nagakanni. Devotees describe the milk poured over his image during abhishekam appearing to turn blue. The temple is just east of Kumbakonam.

Ketu · Keezhperumpallam கேது · கீழப்பெரும்பள்ளம்

South lunar node

A small Chola-era shrine near Poompuhar, where the Kaveri meets the sea. Ketu is shown with a serpent's head and an asura's body, his hands folded in worship of Shiva. Legend says the serpent Vasuki prayed here, which gave Shiva the name Naganatha. Rahu–Ketu transits are celebrated every one and a half years or so.
