Tamil Literature

Two thousand years of Tamil poetry, from love lyrics coded by landscape to a book of 1,330 couplets on how to live. The oldest layer is the Sangam corpus: eight anthologies and ten long poems, eighteen works in all. Eighteen shorter works follow it, the Thirukkural among them. Running beside both are the songs of the Siddhars, the rebel mystics.

🏛️ Why "Sangam"? Later tradition tells of three academies of poets (sangams) that met in Pandya capitals over thousands of years. Scholars treat that chronology as legend, and most date the surviving poems to roughly 300 BCE–300 CE; Kamil Zvelebil favoured 100 BCE–250 CE. The grammar Tolkappiyam sets out the rules the poems follow.

The five landscapes of love

Akam poetry never names its lovers. Instead each poem is set in a landscape (tinai), and the landscape tells the reader which mood of love is meant. Each tinai is named after a flower.

The Eight Anthologies · Ettuthokai

Collections of short and medium poems by hundreds of poets, gathered and arranged by later editors. Counts are the poems that survive.

Natrinai நற்றிணை

400 poems · 9–12 lines

"The good tinai." Love poems across all five landscapes.

Kurunthokai குறுந்தொகை

About 400 poems · 4–8 lines

The short collection. Its compressed love lyrics are among the most translated Tamil poems.

Ainkurunuru ஐங்குறுநூறு

About 500 poems · 3–6 lines

Five poets, one landscape each, with a hundred very short poems apiece.

Kalithokai கலித்தொகை

150 poems

Love poems in the dramatic kali metre

Akananuru அகநானூறு

About 400 poems · 13–31 lines

"The four hundred on the inner world." The longest akam poems.

Purananuru புறநானூறு

About 400 poems

Kings, battles, generosity, death and ethics. It holds the line "Every town is our town, everyone our kin."

Pathitrupathu பதிற்றுப்பத்து

10 decades · 8 survive

Ten poems each in praise of ten Chera kings. The first and last decades are lost.

Paripadal பரிபாடல்

70 composed · 24 survive whole

Songs to Thirumal and Murugan and on the river Vaigai. Hymns and love poetry meet here, early signs of later bhakti.

The Ten Idylls · Pattuppattu

Ten long poems, from about a hundred to almost eight hundred lines. Five are "guide poems" (arruppadai), in which one bard directs another to a generous patron.

Thirukkural திருக்குறள்

Thiruvalluvar · probably the early centuries CE

The best-known of the eighteen shorter works, and the most translated work in Tamil. It has 1,330 couplets in 133 chapters of ten. Each couplet, a kural, is just seven metrical feet long. It speaks to no sect and names no king, which is one reason every age has claimed it.

The Eighteen Shorter Works · Pathinenkilkanakku

Short poems in the venpa metre, mostly c. 100–500 CE. Eleven teach ethics, six are love poetry and one is about war. Naladiyar is the only one by many poets; each of the others has a single author.

The 18 Siddhars

Mystics, alchemists and physicians of the Siddha tradition. They mocked ritual and caste, and they sang of the divine within the body. The names in the list vary between sources; this is the list in the Abithana Chintamani. Siddha medicine still bears their name.

Four Siddhars to know

Thirumoolar திருமூலர்

His Thirumantiram of about 3,000 verses is the tenth book of the Saiva canon (Tirumurai). It holds the famous line "Love is God" (அன்பே சிவம்).

Bogar போகர்

Tradition credits him with the image of Murugan at Palani, made of nine minerals (navapashanam).

Karuvoorar கருவூரார்

Linked by tradition with the building of the Brihadeeswara temple at Thanjavur, which keeps a shrine to him.

Pambatti பாம்பாட்டி

The "snake-charmer" Siddhar. His songs urge the dancing snake, the soul, to rise.
