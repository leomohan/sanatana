Raga Geometry

Carnatic raga and tala, drawn to scale. Pick a raga, press play and watch both wheels move. The twelve notes of the octave sit around the circle with Sa at the top. Each raga's ascent and descent are traced as paths between them.

🎶 Every scale here comes from one of the 72 melakartas. The chart there links back to each raga you can hear on this page. The composers who made these ragas famous are on the Carnatic music page.

In performance every raga also carries gamakas, the oscillations and slides between notes, which no scale drawing can show. Treat each wheel as the raga's skeleton. Your browser plays the sound itself, so turn the volume up and press play.
