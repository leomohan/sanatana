Guru Parampara

A tradition survives only as long as someone teaches it to someone else. Each line below is a parampara, a chain of teachers that its followers count unbroken from its founder to the present. The three lines shown are Shankara's Sringeri seat, the Sri Vaishnava teachers around Ramanuja, and Madhva's pontiffs.

Sringeri (Advaita)Sri Vaishnava (Vishishtadvaita)Madhva (Dvaita)
🕉️ Before the first Jagadguru. Sringeri counts its succession through three earlier lineages. The divine one runs from Sadashiva to Narayana to Brahma. The sages follow: Vasishtha, Shakti, Parashara, Vyasa and Shuka. Then come the teachers Gaudapada and Govinda Bhagavatpada, Shankara's own guru.
