About this map: The 108 Divya Desams are the Vishnu abodes sung by the twelve Azhwars in the Nalayira Divya Prabandham. Of these, 106 are earthly temples concentrated in Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and northern India, with two (Thiruparkadal and Sri Vaikuntham) considered celestial and not mapped here. This map shows a representative set of 20 Divya Desams alongside all 12 Jyotirlingas. The 12 Jyotirlingas are the self-manifested shrines of Shiva scattered across India, from Somnath on the Arabian Sea to Kedarnath in the Himalayas. Coordinates are approximate temple locations; some Divya Desams share the same town and may overlap at this zoom level.

Divya Desams (representative)

  1. Srirangam (Ranganathaswamy) · Tamil Nadu
    Largest functioning Hindu temple; supreme Divya Desam.
  2. Thirukoḻi (Uraiyur) · Tamil Nadu
    Birthplace of Thiruppaan Azhwar.
  3. Thirukkarambanoor · Tamil Nadu
    Uthamar Koil, Tiruchirappalli; rare temple dedicated to the Trimurti.
  4. Pundarikakshan (Thiruvellarai) · Tamil Nadu
    Pallava-era temple with a swastika-shaped tank.
  5. Thiruvengadam (Tirumala) · Andhra Pradesh
    The seven hills of Venkateswara; one of the eight self-manifested shrines.
  6. Kanchipuram (Tirukkacchi) · Tamil Nadu
    City of a thousand temples; multiple Divya Desams here.
  7. Thiruvallikeni (Triplicane) · Tamil Nadu
    Parthasarathy temple, Chennai.
  8. Srivilliputhur · Tamil Nadu
    Birthplace of Periyalvar and Andal.
  9. Thirukkurungudi · Tamil Nadu
    Near Tirunelveli.
  10. Thiruvattar · Tamil Nadu
    Adikesava Perumal temple, Kanyakumari district.
  11. Thiruvananthapuram (Padmanabhaswamy) · Kerala
    Often described as the richest temple in the world.
  12. Thirunavaya · Kerala
    Navamukunda temple on the Bharathapuzha.
  13. Badrinath · Uttarakhand
    One of the Char Dhams; self-manifested shrine.
  14. Muktinath (Thiru Saligramam) · Nepal
    The only Divya Desam outside India; the Kali Gandaki nearby yields the sacred saligrama stones.
  15. Naimisaranyam · Uttar Pradesh
    Ancient forest of sages; self-manifested shrine.
  16. Ayodhya · Uttar Pradesh
    Rama's birthplace; sung by the Azhwars as Thiru Ayodhi.
  17. Vadamathura (Mathura) · Uttar Pradesh
    Krishna's birthplace.
  18. Devaprayag (Thirukkandam) · Uttarakhand
    Raghunathji temple at the confluence that forms the Ganga.
  19. Thirukkovalur · Tamil Nadu
    Where the three earliest Azhwars met and sang the first Thiruvandhadhis.
  20. Dwaraka · Gujarat
    Krishna's kingdom; Dwarkadhish temple.

Jyotirlingas

  1. Somnath · Prabhas Patan, Gujarat
    The first Jyotirlinga, on the Arabian Sea. Destroyed and rebuilt many times.
  2. Mallikarjuna · Srisailam, Andhra Pradesh
    On the Nallamala Hills; both Jyotirlinga and Shakti Peetha.
  3. Mahakaleshwar · Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh
    The only south-facing Jyotirlinga; famous Bhasma Aarti.
  4. Omkareshwar · Mandhata Island, Madhya Pradesh
    On the Narmada; the island resembles the Om symbol.
  5. Kedarnath · Garhwal Himalayas, Uttarakhand
    Highest Jyotirlinga at 3,583 m; open only April–November.
  6. Bhimashankar · Sahyadri Hills, Maharashtra
    Source of the Bhima River; in a wildlife sanctuary.
  7. Kashi Vishwanath · Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh
    Shiva's eternal abode on the Ganges; one of the oldest living cities.
  8. Trimbakeshwar · Nashik, Maharashtra
    Three-faced linga (Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva); origin of the Godavari.
  9. Nageshvara · Dwarka, Gujarat
    Near Dwarka; associated with the demon Daruka. Aundha Nagnath (Maharashtra) also claims this Jyotirlinga.
  10. Baidyanath · Deoghar, Jharkhand
    Also a Shakti Peetha; major pilgrimage in Shravan. Some traditions place this Jyotirlinga at Parli Vaijnath (Maharashtra).
  11. Rameshwaram · Rameswaram Island, Tamil Nadu
    Built by Rama; one of the Char Dhams.
  12. Grishneshwar · Ellora, Maharashtra
    Beside the Ellora caves.
