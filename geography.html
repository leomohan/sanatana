About this map: The 108 Divya Desams are the Vishnu abodes sung by the twelve Azhwars in the Nalayira Divya Prabandham [citation:1]. Of these, 106 are earthly temples concentrated in Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and northern India, with two (Thiruparkadal and Sri Vaikuntham) considered celestial and not mapped here . The 12 Jyotirlingas are the self-manifested shrines of Shiva scattered across India, from Somnath on the Arabian Sea to Kedarnath in the Himalayas [citation:2][citation:8]. Coordinates are approximate temple locations; some Divya Desams share the same town and may overlap at this zoom level.
